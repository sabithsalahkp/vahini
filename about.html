OUR STORY

Rooted in Manjeri.
Focused on your story.

Based in Manjeri, Kerala, Vahini Studio works with couples and families to document their most meaningful celebrations. From the anticipation before a ceremony to the last moments of a reception, we aim for photographs that feel personal and films that bring the day back to life.

Our work covers wedding celebrations, pre-wedding sessions, portraits and family events. We believe the strongest images come from a balance of thoughtful composition and genuine, unplanned emotion.

HOW WE WORK
